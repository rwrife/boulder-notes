Boulder Notes is a private, offline bouldering journal for recording gym sessions, routes, attempts, beta notes, and optional wall photos on phone, tablet, and Windows.

Create a gym, log a session, add routes with gym-specific grades, colors and wall locations, and record sends, failures and your own difficulty notes. Keep past attempts and routes when a gym resets its wall. Pick wall photos yourself; the app stores them locally on your device. No account, ads, analytics, or cloud sync.

Portable backup export and import are planned, not yet available. Boulder Notes is a personal log, not coaching or medical advice.
